THE GROUND RULES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

What Versa collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

01 Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how the Versa team handles personal information when you use playversa.fun. Contact @PlayVersa_ on X about privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. You can ask for a private contact method before sharing sensitive information; do not post identification documents, wallet secrets or account tokens publicly.

02 Information you provide

We process your wallet public address, authentication messages and signatures when you sign in; account identifiers and creation dates; your display name, username, biography, favourite games, social links and challenge preferences; and avatar or cover images you upload. We also store invitation details, agreed game rules, participants, status changes, community messages and abuse reports. Do not put sensitive personal information into a public profile, message or game rule.

03 X sign-in when enabled

X sign-in is being added and is not active until enabled in the service. If you choose it, X will ask you to authorize access. We intend to request the X user ID, username, display name and profile image needed to create or link your Versa account. Authentication also involves authorization codes and access tokens. We do not request permission to post, send direct messages or follow accounts on your behalf, and do not request your email address for this sign-in. Adding an X profile link manually does not verify ownership. We will update this notice if the implemented sign-in collects additional information.

04 Technical information and local storage

Our servers and hosting providers process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser information and request timestamps to deliver pages, secure the service and investigate failures. Versa uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in; the current wallet session expires after seven days unless you sign out earlier. Your browser also stores your recent wallet choice and quiz question rotation. Wallet connection authorization may be held in memory during a session. The current frontend does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Hosting and linked services may process technical information under their own policies.

05 Why we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, save profile changes, show public player profiles, deliver and track invitations, operate chat and practice quizzes, handle reports and requests, prevent abuse and protect the service. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing the service you request, legitimate interests in security and reliable operation, consent for optional connected features where appropriate, and compliance with legal obligations. You can browse public pages without creating an account; account details are needed for features that save data or communicate with players.

06 What other people can see

Your username, display name, biography, profile images, favourite games, social links and profile creation date can be public. Community messages are visible to people who can view chat. Invitations may be accessible through their links, and private invitations are limited by the service’s access controls. Do not treat an invitation link as a confidential file vault. Your wallet address is stored for account authentication and is not displayed in the current public profile response. A blockchain address and any onchain activity are independently public, and information you share can allow others to link your identity and address.

07 Service providers and other sharing

Our frontend is hosted through GitHub Pages and our backend through Hostinger. These providers process data needed to serve and operate the site. Wallet providers and X process information when you use their connection or sign-in services. We may disclose relevant information to comply with valid legal requests, investigate abuse or protect people and the service. Public content is shared as described above. We do not sell personal information or use X data to train AI models. External sites linked by users have their own privacy practices.

08 Retention and deletion

We keep account and profile information while needed to provide your account. Invitation history, messages, reports and relevant security records are retained according to their operational purpose, unresolved complaints and any applicable legal obligations. The service does not currently apply one automatic deletion period to every record type. Signing out deletes the current server session, but does not delete your account or content. Clearing browser storage removes local wallet preferences and quiz rotation; it does not erase server records. You may request account or content deletion through our contact channel. Backup copies and limited security or legally required records may remain until they are no longer needed; deletion does not remove copies other users made or public blockchain records.

09 Your choices and rights

You can edit profile details in the service and choose which optional social links or images to share. You can disconnect your wallet, sign out and, once X sign-in is available, revoke Versa’s authorization in your X account settings. Revoking a provider does not by itself delete your Versa account. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact @PlayVersa_ on X; we may verify account control before acting. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations or other people’s rights. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

10 Security and international processing

We use measures such as authenticated sessions, signature verification, access controls and encrypted HTTPS connections to protect data. No online system can guarantee complete security. Hosting, authentication and wallet providers may process information in countries other than your own, where laws can differ. Provider locations and applicable transfer safeguards depend on the service involved; contact us for available information. We do not claim that publishing this policy alone establishes compliance in every jurisdiction.

11 Children and future features

Account and interactive features are intended for adults aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate. Paid play, escrow, automated settlement and dispute evidence uploads are not currently enabled. We will describe any additional data collection before enabling such features.

12 Policy updates

We will update this page and its date when practices change, and provide an appropriate notice for material changes. Privacy questions and complaints can be directed to @PlayVersa_ on X.

Questions? Contact @PlayVersa_ on X.
